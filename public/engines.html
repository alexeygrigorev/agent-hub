Engine accounts

Add an account once from the CLI. Its engine and profile appear on connected machines automatically.

hub engines add --provider codex --label personal --auth-file ~/.codex/auth.json

hub engines add --provider gemini --label google installs Gemini everywhere; authorize each machine below through native Google sign-in.
